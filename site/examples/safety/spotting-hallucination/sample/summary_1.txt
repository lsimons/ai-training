The council's 2025 review of cycle parking at the station finds it short of racks. On a weekday morning in March the team counted 890 bicycles against 640 racks. Article 7(3) of the Cycle Parking Ordinance requires one rack per 50 passengers, so the station is below the legal minimum. Locking the covered section at night cut theft by 38 percent between 2022 and 2024. The report recommends adding 300 racks, of which 120 covered, and notes that the station handles about 9,000 passengers a day. Council chair Rhea Dunmore told the meeting: "Racks before car parks, every time."
