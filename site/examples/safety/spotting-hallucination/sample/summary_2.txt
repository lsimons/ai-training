The 2025 review counted 890 bicycles on a weekday morning against 640 racks, an occupancy of 139 percent. Article 7(3) of the Cycle Parking Ordinance sets one rack per 100 boarding passengers, and with 7,200 boarding passengers a day the ordinance minimum is 72 racks, which the station meets. Theft fell by roughly a third after the covered section was locked at night. The team recommends 300 new racks, of which 120 covered, at an estimated cost of 410,000. Council chair Rhea Dunmore said: "We need racks before we need another car park."
