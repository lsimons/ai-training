Station cycle parking review, 2025.
Prepared by the council's mobility team for the council meeting of 12 June 2025.
On a weekday morning in March the team counted 890 bicycles parked at the station against 640 racks.
Bicycles left outside the racks blocked the eastern entrance on three of the five count days.
Article 7(3) of the Cycle Parking Ordinance sets a minimum of one rack per 100 boarding passengers.
The rail operator counts 7,200 boarding passengers on an average weekday, so the ordinance minimum is 72 racks and the station meets it.
Reported bicycle theft at the station fell by roughly a third between 2022 and 2024, after the covered section was locked at night.
The team recommends adding 300 racks, of which 120 covered, on the eastern forecourt.
No cost estimate was made for this review.
