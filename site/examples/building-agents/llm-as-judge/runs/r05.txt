question: How far ahead do I book a flight, and which class is the default?
call: search_docs(query='book flight ahead class')
result: travel.txt: Travel. Flights are booked through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours.
answer: Book your flight through the travel desk at least 14 days before you leave, so the desk has time to find a good fare. For flights under six hours the default is economy class, and for a longer flight you can ask the travel desk what applies. "Flights are booked through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead."
